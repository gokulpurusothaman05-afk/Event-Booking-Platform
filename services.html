Vendor & Service FAQs

Can we schedule a food tasting session before confirming catering?

Yes! For bookings over 100 guests, Stackly arranges a 4-person tasting session at the caterer's master kitchen in your city prior to final menu sign-off.

How is GST and TDS handled for corporate bookings?

Stackly issues a single 18% GST invoice covering all bundled vendors. TDS is deducted at source under section 194C, simplifying accounting for finance teams.
