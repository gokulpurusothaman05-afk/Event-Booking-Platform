Venue Booking FAQs

What is the advance booking and escrow policy?

You lock your preferred dates with a 25% deposit. Your funds remain in Stackly's RBI-compliant escrow account until 48 hours prior to the event, after our on-ground inspection is satisfied.

Can we bring our own outside catering team?

Selected venues allow outside master chefs subject to an FSSAI certificate review and kitchen coordination deposit. Alternatively, you can choose from our 40+ pre-vetted regional catering partners.

What happens if city noise regulations require music to stop at 10 PM?

In accordance with Indian municipal laws, outdoor amplification ceases at 10:00 PM. Our listed spaces feature acoustic-damped indoor ballrooms and lounges so your celebrations continue uninterrupted.
