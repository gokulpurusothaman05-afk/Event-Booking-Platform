Cancellation & Refund Terms

What is the cancellation timeline and refund schedule?

Cancellations made 45+ days before the event receive a 90% refund of the deposit (10% platform processing fee). Cancellations within 15–44 days receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 14 days forfeit the deposit, but credit can be transferred to a future date.

What happens in the event of severe weather or force majeure?

For open-air clifftop lawns in Goa or desert camps in Rajasthan, Stackly includes mandatory weather insurance. If municipal authorities issue a red weather alert, our contracts allow free rescheduling within 90 days.
